## What PRISM contributes

- **Decisions for material of unknown history.** The degradation state of post-consumer PET is inferred
  from its ATR-FTIR/NIR spectral fingerprint alone; no reprocessing history is needed.
- **A mechanical↔chemical transition boundary.** Technical (WP1), chemical (WP2) and sustainability (WP4)
  criteria are placed on one degradation axis and fused into a mechanical, hybrid or chemical
  recommendation.
- **Physics-informed inference with honest uncertainty.** Degradation physics constrains the inference
  (a physics-enforced neural network with a B-PINN-style band in the deployed tool); the uncertainty is
  carried through to a decision-stability measure that flags samples near a route boundary.
- **From spectrum to mechanical performance.** The same inferred state predicts the full mechanical
  profile through a spectral→mechanical reference library.
- **From decision to value.** Each recommendation is linked to the end-use markets the material can still
  serve, with carbon and value estimates.
- **Transparent, reproducible decisions.** Criterion weights are exposed and their influence shown; every
  assessment is saved as a replayable record of inputs, versions and predictions.

In the demonstrator, every parameter and prediction is an illustrative placeholder (see `SCIENCE_NOTES.md`).
